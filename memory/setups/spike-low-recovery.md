# Setup: Spike Low Recovery

**Hypothesis:** A panic spike low followed by tight consolidation traps short
sellers who entered during the impulse. When CVD turns and the M5 close
reclaims its fast MA, the trapped party covers — fueling the bounce.

## Lenses

- **Flow:** CVD goes deeply negative during the spike, then divergence emerges
  — price holds the low while CVD prints higher. Volume spike on the spike bar
  is required (>2x the 20-bar average).
- **Structure:** The spike low must be the lowest point of the active dealing
  range. A bullish FVG (3-bar gap) inside the consolidation zone increases
  conviction.
- **Context:** Works in any session. Higher conviction during NY-overlap and
  London open due to liquidity depth.
- **Intent:** Funding rate flipping from positive to negative during the spike
  is a tell — longs got flushed, the next move is a squeeze of new shorts.

## Trapped party

Short sellers entered during the impulsive drop. Pain accumulates as price
consolidates above their entry, then accelerates as price breaks the
consolidation high.

## Kill thesis

The single observable that invalidates: **M5 close back below the spike low**.
If that prints, the bounce thesis is dead — the market is committing to
markdown, not reversing.

## Default risk

- SL: 25 basis points below entry (entry just above consolidation high)
- TP1: 15 bp (scale 25%)
- TP2: 30 bp (scale 25%)
- TP3: 60 bp (scale 25%, trail remainder)
- Minimum R:R: 3:1 from entry to TP3
